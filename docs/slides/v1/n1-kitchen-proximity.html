why spatial won

The episodic layer captures what people say and do, which gives high counts to whoever talks most — Katrina is loud in many scenes. Proximity is a different signal: who is physically beside the wearer. Spatial triples encode it directly. Without the spatial layer, no episodic action verb says "Shure stood next to me", so the model picks the wrong person.
