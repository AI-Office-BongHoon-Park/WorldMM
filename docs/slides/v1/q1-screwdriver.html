why spatial won

Episodic captures speech and small motions but never tags the *first* user of the screwdriver. The spatial layer made it concrete: the camera wearer was behind Alice — placing Alice in the natural position to be doing the close-up screw work. Combined with the episodic keyword "tighten screws", the agent committed to Alice.

pattern

When the episodic layer is dense on dialogue but silent on *who-touched-what*, the spatial layer's relative-position triples (behind, next_to, in_front_of) act as a tie-breaker among candidates.
